KiloGames

Something Different

We're trying something different: mini-MegaGames, or "KiloGames"! Smaller, ~12-20 player games over 2-4h, every couple months. We might have smaller versions of MegaGames we've run, or hidden role games like Blood on the Clocktower, murder mysteries, or other fun stuff! If you have any ideas, be sure to let us know on our Contact page.

The next game we're running is on October 24th. We're currently looking at venues and will update this once one is booked.
